# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive collaboration environment.

Be respectful, focus criticism on the work, assume good faith while requiring
evidence, and protect private operational and security information. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential exposure, and sustained disruptive
behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns through a private repository security report when
privacy is required. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and
restrict participation to protect contributors and the project.
